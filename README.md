# The Money Stroll

A small Three.js animation: a bald, bearded man in a navy shirt and jeans strolls and whistles around a plaza while Canadian-style bills, loonies and toonies shake off him like leaves. A running tally climbs at a $150,000-a-year pace (spent over 10 hours a day), about a cent a second.

He strolls toward the viewer, angled left or right. Drag anywhere sideways to spin him and send him another way; tap him to shake loose a burst of money. Phones get a lighter graphics mode automatically.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view it on GitHub Pages.
